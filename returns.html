Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. 30-Day Return Window

We want you to love everything you buy from Kanak. If you're not completely satisfied, you may return most items within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Because your satisfaction is gold.

2. Return Conditions

To be eligible for a return, items must meet the following conditions:

  • Products must be unopened, unused, and in original packaging with all seals intact.
  • Cosmetics and skincare products must have the safety seal unbroken (for hygiene reasons).
  • The return request must be initiated within 30 days of the delivery date.
  • Items must be returned with the original invoice or order confirmation.
  • Gift sets and bundled items must be returned as complete sets.

Non-returnable items: Opened cosmetics, skincare, or personal care products (for hygiene and safety reasons). Items marked as final sale or clearance.

3. Damaged or Defective Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@kanakbeauty.com with photos of the issue. We will arrange a free return pickup and send a replacement or issue a full refund — whichever you prefer.

4. Return Process

Initiating a return is simple:

  1. Email us at hello@kanakbeauty.com with your order number and reason for return.
  2. Our team will reply within 24 hours with return authorization and pickup details.
  3. Pack the item securely in its original packaging with the invoice.
  4. Our courier partner will pick up the package from your address at no cost to you.
  5. Once received and inspected, we will process your refund.

5. Refund Timeline

Refunds are processed within these timeframes:

  • Inspection: 1–2 business days after we receive your return.
  • Approval: You'll receive a confirmation email once approved.
  • Refund to original payment method: 5–7 business days (credit card / netbanking).
  • UPI refunds: 2–3 business days.
  • COD refunds: 7–10 business days via bank transfer (you'll need to provide account details).

Shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error (damaged, defective, or incorrect item). Return shipping is free for all valid returns.

6. Exchanges

We currently do not offer direct exchanges. If you'd like a different shade or product, please initiate a return and place a new order. For shade-related exchanges, contact our team and we'll help you find the right match before you reorder.

7. Cancellations

You can cancel your order within 2 hours of placing it — before it's processed for shipping. Email us immediately at hello@kanakbeauty.com with your order number. Once shipped, cancellations are treated as returns.
