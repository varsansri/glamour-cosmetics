3. Order Tracking

Once your order is dispatched, you will receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can track your order on our Track Order page by entering your order ID and email, or directly on the courier partner's website.

4. Cash on Delivery (COD)

Cash on Delivery is available for most pin codes across India. A ₹49 COD charge applies to all COD orders. COD is not available for orders above ₹10,000. Our delivery partner will collect the payment in cash when your package arrives. Please have the exact amount ready.

5. Shipping Partners

We partner with India's most reliable courier services:

  • BlueDart — Standard & Express shipping
  • Delhivery — Standard shipping to all pin codes
  • India Post — Remote and rural areas

6. Undelivered Packages

If a package is returned to us due to an incorrect address, three failed delivery attempts, or refusal to accept, we will contact you. Re-shipment charges will apply. If we cannot reach you within 7 days, the order will be refunded minus shipping and handling charges of ₹100.
