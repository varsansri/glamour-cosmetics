Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

1. Introduction

At GScosmatics Beauty Pvt. Ltd. ("GScosmatics," "we," "us," or "our"), your privacy is gold. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, make a purchase, or interact with us. By using our site, you agree to the terms of this policy.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly:

  • Personal details: Name, email address, phone number, and shipping/billing address when you place an order or create an account.
  • Payment information: Credit card details and UPI IDs — these are processed securely by our payment partner (Razorpay) and never stored on our servers.
  • Communications: Messages you send via our contact form, email, or WhatsApp.

We also collect information automatically:

  • Usage data: Pages visited, time spent, items viewed, and interactions on our site.
  • Device information: Browser type, IP address, device type, and operating system.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders, including shipping and returns.
  • Send order confirmations, tracking updates, and delivery notifications.
  • Respond to your inquiries and provide customer support.
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent — you can opt out anytime).
  • Improve our website, products, and shopping experience.
  • Prevent fraud and maintain the security of our platform.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience. These include:

  • Essential cookies: Required for core functionality like the shopping cart and checkout.
  • Analytics cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our site so we can improve it.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your preferences, such as recently viewed items.

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. However, some features like the shopping cart may not function properly without them.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We may share your data only with:

  • Service providers: Payment processors (Razorpay), shipping carriers (BlueDart, Delhivery), and email service providers — all bound by confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal compliance: When required by law, court order, or to protect our rights.
  • Business transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

6. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including 128-bit SSL encryption, secure servers, and regular security audits. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We strive to protect your data but cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Under Indian data protection laws, you have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: Correct any inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Erasure: Request deletion of your personal data (subject to legal retention requirements).
  • Object: Opt out of marketing communications or restrict processing.
  • Portability: Receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@kanakbeauty.com. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Continued use of our site after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
