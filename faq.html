Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Kanak — your golden beauty companion.

Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days across India. Express delivery arrives in 2–3 business days. We ship via BlueDart, Delhivery, and India Post. You'll receive tracking details via email once your order is dispatched.

We offer a 30-day easy return policy. If you're not satisfied with your purchase, you can return unopened and unused products within 30 days for a full refund. Visit our Return Policy page for full details.

Absolutely. 100% of our products are cruelty-free. We never test on animals at any stage of production. Our formulations are also paraben-free, sulfate-free, and phthalate-free. Many products are vegan — check individual product pages for vegan labels.

We accept all major payment methods: UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Rupay), netbanking, and digital wallets. Cash on Delivery (COD) is also available with an additional ₹49 charge. All online payments are secured via Razorpay with 128-bit SSL encryption.

Yes. All Kanak products are dermatologist-tested and formulated to be gentle. We avoid common irritants like synthetic fragrances, parabens, and sulfates. However, we always recommend doing a patch test before trying any new skincare product. If you have specific allergies, please check the ingredients list on each product page or contact us at hello@kanakbeauty.com.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email and SMS. You can also visit our Track Order page, enter your order ID and email, and get real-time status updates.

Currently, we only ship within India. We're working hard to bring Kanak to international customers. Sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know when we launch international shipping. In the meantime, follow us on Instagram for global updates.
